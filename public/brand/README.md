# Original OMOBA identity assets

`omoba-logo.png` is copied byte-for-byte from the game repository's root
`omoba-logo.png`. Preserve the full mark, its colors and proportions. The app
icon is the same source, and the header pairs it with readable OMOBA lettering.

Fonts are copied from `omoba-bevy/client/assets/ui/verdant/fonts/` with their
original SIL Open Font Licenses. Barlow Condensed Bold is the English display
face. Inter Regular / SemiBold is the interface face and the Cyrillic fallback.
The landing retains its existing Plus Jakarta Sans body font.

The illustration under `/art/supporter/` is the existing reviewed Higgsfield
asset. Its provenance and concept-art limitation remain alongside it. Actual
asset/model previews and skill atlases come from the reviewed game catalog.

Full visual standards: `wotori/decks/decks/omoba-brandbook-2026/`.
